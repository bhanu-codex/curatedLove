💝 curatedLove
The AI-Powered Boutique Concierge
curatedLove is a high-end personal gifting assistant designed to bridge the gap between generic suggestions and thoughtful, "bespoke" experiences. By analyzing the recipient's age, gender, and the emotional depth of your relationship, it generates 3 distinct strategies for every occasion.

✨ Why curatedLove?
Direct AI chats can be overwhelming. curatedLove filters the noise to provide:

🎁 The Safe Bet: Timeless, reliable gifts that never miss.

🚀 The Wildcard: Bold, creative, and unexpected surprises.

🌟 The Experience: Memory-making moments over material things.

🛠️ Built With
Streamlit - For the modern, responsive "Boutique" UI.

Ollama - Powering the local intelligence via phi3:mini.

Python - The backbone of the logic and personalization engine.

🚀 Unique Features
Emotional Intelligence: Adjusts card message tone based on relationship depth (Professional 🤝 vs. Super Close 🤞).

Age & Gender Apt: Ensures suggestions are socially and practically appropriate for the recipient.

Local & Private: Runs entirely on your machine; your loved ones' data stays yours.

One-Click Utility: Integrated "Copy Message" buttons for immediate use.
